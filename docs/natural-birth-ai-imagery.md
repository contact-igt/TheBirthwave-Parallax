Indian casting, warm ivory/dusty-rose/sage/natural-wood daylight palette, no

`public/images/services/` and replace the matching `gradient` entry in

confirmed service illustration set — no Natural Birth visual here duplicates

confirmed), emergency scenes, fabricated documents, any text/logo/watermark.
